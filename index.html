About

Chief Investment Officer at Quantecko, working at the meeting point of derivatives, data and portfolio construction.

Alam leads investment at Quantecko, where he builds portfolios that combine several derivative strategies into a single institutional-grade book. He has more than five years of experience in derivative finance. Outside Quantecko, he managed a 10M portfolio for three years.

He builds and validates systematic signals for equity and derivatives books. Most of his work sits between the data and the portfolio: cleaning point-in-time datasets, specifying hypotheses before looking at returns, and stress-testing results with purged cross-validation, multiple-testing corrections and realistic cost models.

A core part of that work is backtesting derivatives strategies across more than 15 years of options and futures data, covering several volatility regimes and market crises so that results reflect how a strategy behaves through full cycles, not a single favourable period.

He also uses fixed income instruments in dual trades, pairing them with derivative positions to put collateral to work and lift overall portfolio return.
